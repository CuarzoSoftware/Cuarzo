Detailed Description

Data offered to this client by a drag & drop session (wl_data_offer).

Received through the MSurface::onDNDEnter / onDNDMotion / onDNDLeave / onDNDDrop signals of the surface under the pointer. Only valid until the leave or drop signal returns.

To be a valid drop target, call accept() with one of the mimeTypes() (and setActions()) when the drag enters or moves. On drop, read the data with receive() and call finish().
