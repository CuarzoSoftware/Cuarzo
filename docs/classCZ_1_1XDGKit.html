◆ desktopEntry()

std::shared_ptr< const XDGDesktopEntry > desktopEntry ( const std::string & appId)
noexcept

Finds the desktop entry (.desktop file) of an application.

Looks for <appId>.desktop (and its lowercase variant, which covers most X11 WM_CLASS based ids) in $XDG_DATA_HOME/applications and every $XDG_DATA_DIRS/applications. Results, including misses, are cached.

Returns
The entry, or nullptr if none was found.

◆ desktopEntryForWindow()

std::shared_ptr< const XDGDesktopEntry > desktopEntryForWindow ( const std::string & windowAppId)
noexcept

Finds the desktop entry of the app a window belongs to, from the window's app id (Wayland app_id or X11 WM_CLASS).

They usually match the entry's id, but not always: e.g. Electron and Flatpak apps (Discord's windows are "discord", its entry com.discordapp.Discord). In order:

  1. The entry whose StartupWMClass is exactly windowAppId (checked first, like GNOME and KDE do: an app's own entry may say so even if another entry has that id).
  2. desktopEntry() of windowAppId.
  3. The entry whose StartupWMClass matches it ignoring case.

The StartupWMClass of every entry is read once (and again on a miss, at most every 30 s, for apps installed meanwhile). Results, including misses, are cached.

Returns
The entry, or nullptr if none was found.

◆ desktopEntriesForWindow()

const std::vector< std::shared_ptr< const XDGDesktopEntry > > & desktopEntriesForWindow ( const std::string & windowAppId)
noexcept

Every desktop entry a window with app id windowAppId may belong to, the most likely first (see desktopEntryForWindow()).

There may be several: e.g. a Flatpak's entry and a user's own entry for the same app (both declaring the WM class, or one by its id). A dock may prefer the one pinned or running.
