Detailed Description

An output (a connected display) as wlr-output-management shows it, enabled or not.

Listed by MOutputManager (MApp::ext, requires MOutputManagerExt). Created and destroyed by the manager: it's announced with MOutputManager::onAdded once its initial props are known, and destroyed right after MOutputManager::onRemoved (e.g. when the display is unplugged).

Unlike MScreen (wl_output), disabled outputs are listed too, along with every mode they support. To change how they're set up, see MOutputManager::apply().
