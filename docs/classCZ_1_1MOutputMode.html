Detailed Description

A display mode an output supports (wlr-output-management), see MOutputHead::Props::modes.

Created and destroyed by its head (MOutputHead). Its props don't change: when the compositor removes a mode, it's taken out of its head's modes (MOutputHead::CHModes) and destroyed.
