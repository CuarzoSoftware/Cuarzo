Detailed Description

A subscription of a callback to a CZSignal.

Created by CZSignal::subscribe(). A listener is owned by both its signal and its owner object: it is destroyed automatically when either of them is destroyed. Deleting it earlier unsubscribes the callback. It is safe to do so (or to subscribe new listeners) while the signal is being emitted.
