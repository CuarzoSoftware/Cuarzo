Detailed Description

A frame requested by an image capture session.

The client attaches a buffer (shm or dma-buf) of LImageCaptureSession::bufferSize(), tells what it already has (clientDamage()) and asks to capture. Louvre fills it once there's something new (the first frame of a session immediately), copying only what changed, and sends ready.

Output frames are handled entirely by Louvre. Toplevel frames are rendered by LToplevelRole::imageCaptureFrameRequest(): to render them yourself, draw paintRegion() into surface() and call ready(), or call fail().

inlinenoexcept

What the client asks to be updated, in buffer coordinates.

The client's buffers may be stale there (e.g. reused buffers), in addition to the session's damage. Louvre already considers it in paintRegion() and when copying.

◆ surface()

std::shared_ptr< RSurface > surface ( )
noexcept

Where to render the frame (toplevel sources).

Its geometry maps buffer pixels 1:1 (viewport and destination are the buffer rect). It's the client's own buffer for dma-buf buffers, or an image whose content persists for shm buffers.

Returns
nullptr on failure (then call fail()).
