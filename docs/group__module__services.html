Services

Overview

Clients of the system and session services a desktop shell needs: Wi-Fi, audio, status indicators, media players and notifications.

Header#include <CZ/Services/SVWiFi.h>
pkg-configcz-services
Mesondependency('cz-services')
Depends onCore, libsystemd (sd-bus)

Each service is a class of its own, independent from the others: it talks to its service over D-Bus off the UI thread (see CZBusWorker) and tells when its state changed with signals. Without the service (or its hardware) its state says it isn't available.

Like CZCore, each one is shared by the process: GetOrMake() returns the instance (made on first use), which lives while some shared pointer to it does.

Main Classes

  • SVWiFi: NetworkManager's Wi-Fi. Turning it on and off, the networks in range (SVWiFiNetwork), joining (asking for passwords), forgetting them and their Auto-Join.
  • SVAudio: the audio devices of PipeWire or PulseAudio. The default output and input, or just some of their channels (SVAudioSelection), their volume, the input level and playing sounds.
  • SVStatus: the network, volume and battery state as symbolic icon names, for a bar's indicators.
  • SVNowPlaying: what MPRIS media players are playing, and controlling them.
  • SVNotificationsClient sends desktop notifications (SVNotification) and controls Gazette; SVNotificationsServer is the protocol side of a notifications daemon.
