Detailed Description

Drag & drop icon role for surfaces.

The LDNDIconRole role is used during drag & drop sessions. See LDND::icon().

Destructor of the LDNDIconRole class.

Invoked after LCompositor::onAnticipatedObjectDestruction().

Warning
The surface() handle always remains valid during the destructor call. However, LSurface::role() returns nullptr because LSurface::roleChanged() is notified beforehand and requires the role to be valid.
