Detailed Description

Nine-patch image node.

Splits an image into nine regions defined by a center rect (see setCenter()): the four corners keep their size, the four edges and the center stretch to fill the node's bounds. Each region can be hidden individually with setRegionVisibility(). Nothing is rendered while no image is set or the center rect is invalid (negative origin or exceeding the image's logical size).

◆ Region

enum Region

The nine patch regions.

TL, TR, BR and BL are the fixed-size corners; L, T, R and B the stretched edges; C the stretched center. Region_Last is the number of regions.
